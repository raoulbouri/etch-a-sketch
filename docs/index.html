This is live physics, not a recording: MuJoCo runs in your browser, and the simulated Kinova Gen3 grips each knob and turns it through contact.

The arm draws open loop from the toy's nominal gear ratio and backlash, which are placeholder values until measured on the real toy. The board position is read from the simulator here; on the bench it comes from the camera.
